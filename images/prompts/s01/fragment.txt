High three-quarter angle, looking down: a small fragment of a modest French apartment entrance hall from 1984 floats alone in darkness, like a diorama. Only a patch of tiled floor, a piece of orange wall with a front door, and a ceiling lamp hanging on its wire from nothing, casting a single cone of warm light onto the floor. No people: the centre of the lit floor is empty, characters will stand there. Some areas are missing, as if they were never remembered: holes of pure black in the wall and in the floor. The edges of the fragment crumble into floating blocks of pixels and dust in the dark.
