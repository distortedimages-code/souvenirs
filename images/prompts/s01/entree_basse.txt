Low-angle shot from the eyes of a small child, looking up, in a modest French apartment entrance hall in 1984. A single round ceiling lamp glows in the upper centre, like a halo. A front door and a coat rack, out of focus. No people: the centre of the image, under the lamp, stays empty for a character who will appear there. The edges of the image dissolve into scattered blocks of pixels and warm darkness, as if the memory were incomplete.
