Concept art key frame for a narrative video game about memories. Pixel art with modern cinematic lighting and a strong depth of field: pixel textures on real volumes, tilt-shift blur, soft volumetric light, fine film grain.
High three-quarter angle, looking down: a small fragment of a French apartment entrance hall from 1984 floats alone in warm darkness, like a diorama. Only a patch of tiled floor, a piece of orange wall with a front door, and a ceiling lamp hanging on its wire from nothing, casting a single cone of warm light. In the light, tiny pixel art characters: a woman seen from behind, her face hidden, carrying a toddler who holds a tiny white toy plane; a grandmother with a grey bun facing them; a small brown-haired boy between them, looking up at the toy.
The edges of the fragment crumble into floating pixels and dust in the dark. Only the toy plane is pure white; everything else is amber and brown. Quiet, poetic, bold composition.
