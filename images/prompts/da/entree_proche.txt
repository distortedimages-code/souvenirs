Concept art key frame for a narrative video game about memories. Pixel art with modern cinematic lighting and a strong depth of field: pixel textures on real volumes, tilt-shift blur, soft volumetric light, fine film grain.
Low-angle shot from the eyes of a two-year-old child held in someone's arms, in a French apartment entrance hall in 1984. An elderly grandmother with grey hair gathered in a bun leans toward the camera, smiling warmly. A single round ceiling lamp glows just behind her head, like a halo. In the sharp foreground, the child's small hands hold a little white toy jet airplane with a simple invented design and no logo: it is the only pure white, fully saturated detail in the image. At the bottom edge, the top of a four-year-old boy's head with brown hair, looking up. The left edge is cut by the dark, blurred sleeve of the adult who carries the child.
Everything else is desaturated warm amber and deep brown. The image is a floating memory fragment: its edges dissolve into scattered pixels and warm darkness. Intimate, poetic, bold composition.
