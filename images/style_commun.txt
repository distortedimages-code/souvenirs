Minimalist retro pixel art for a narrative video game about memories. Very low detail: large flat areas of colour, simple geometric shapes, clean silhouettes, no texture, no pattern, no small objects, no clutter. A tiny palette of about twelve warm, faded colours. Soft light and a gentle haze, like a half-remembered dream. Big chunky pixels. Side view, flat orthographic perspective, like the background of an early 1990s 2D adventure game.
